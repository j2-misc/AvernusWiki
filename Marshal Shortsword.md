*Wondrous Item, Rare*
This shortsword deals an extra 2d6 radiant damage to fiends and undead. In addition, when you hit a creature with this attack, you can mark the creature and marshal your allies. A creature marked this way takes an additional 1d6 damage from your allies’ attacks and spells. Once you mark a creature this way, you cannot do so again until you finish a long rest.
