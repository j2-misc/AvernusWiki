# Synopsis
Also known as the Riders of Elturel, the Hellriders are an elite cavalry unit serving as the primary military force of [[Elturel]].

# The Old Hellriders
The former military force tasked with protecting [[Idyllglen]]. They followed [[Zariel]] into [[Baator|Hell]] where they were quickly overwhelmed by [[The Blood War]]. All who couldn't escape and didn't succumb to [[Zariel]]'s corruption were slain.
