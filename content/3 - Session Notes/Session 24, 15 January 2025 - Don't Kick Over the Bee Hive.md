# Verbose
The air was thick with tension. They parked beside [[The River Styx]] and Olanthius spoke with Ilsa-vyx.

He asked, "Is everything alright? You look down."

Ilsa-vyx answered shortly, "Ilsa-vyx always looks down, Ilsa-vyx is short!"

"Wouldn't you be looking up...? Nevermind"

"Ilsa-vyx is just feeling a lot of feelings right now. Very upset." After a moment she explained, "we made a bad deal."

"With [[Bel]]?"

"Yeah..."

Olanthius was furious.

Ilsa-vyx explained the situation, that the deal was to help [[Elturel]] and that part of it was to destroy [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]].

"I don't understand what you'd ever want from a [[Devils|Devil]]'s deal. They will always twist your words."

"That's what Ilsa-vyx is always saying!" She sighed, "god, what would Auntie Octavia think..."

"You have to set your troop straight."

Ilsa-vyx asked if he ever needed to straighten out his own troops. He recalled the choice he and his men were presented under [[Zariel]]'s new reign. Death or obedience; he understood the need to survive, but there is no life under a Devil. Ilsa-vyx looked to the others.

Mekhi sat uncomfortably in the driver's seat, Kiarin next to him, clutching the Hidden Lord tightly.

"You need to get your troop in order," Olanthius concluded.

Ilsa-vyx pushed back against this, that everyone in the party did what they did better than her. That Mekhi was a great warrior, Evryn a flawless storyteller, and that Kiarin did everything well. "If Ilsa-vyx had known at the beginning it would be death or servitude she might have made a different choice..."

"Don't demean yourself. You are a [[Hellriders|Hellrider]], for [[Torm]]'s sake!"

"... Ilsa-vyx thinks she sees the boat."
## The Barge
[[Koh Tam]]'s barge pulled into view. On board were two additional figures: a [[Bearded Devil]] and [[Tiax]]--the gnome from [[The Tower of Urm]]. They boarded and Koh Tam shrunk down their vehicle, placing it on the barge with them. Tiax happily explained he got a new job.

Koh Tam asked where they were headed.

Evryn explained, "we actually came to seek you out. There is a person among [[The Hells' Exiles]] who is ill--my mother, [[Celosia]]."

"Celosia," he said, "I know that name. I have spoken with her once or twice." He dismissed his hired hands to the lower decks. "Proceed."

"She's been using a lot of magic since she's gotten here, helping people out and I think she overdid it this last time. She fell unconscious and hasn't been able to wake up."

"Do you know of her true nature?"

"Yes," he half-sighed, "it feels like she shouldn't... be in [[Baator|Hell]] for a prolonged period of time, much less doing that sort of magic."

"You are correct. The very essence of this plane is a poison to [[Celestial|Celestials]]. I do not have the power to do what you ask, but I know of a place where you may find something to help her. I can take you to a truly abhorent torrid place known as a [[Hellwasp Nest]]. A nest made from the slain remnants of [[Celestial|Celestials]] within the Hells. If you can harvest that essence, you could use it to restore Celosia, but of course getting it will be difficult. [[Hellwasps]] are parasites, monsters born of the Hells that feed on divine essence. The way those creatures live sickens me. You would be doing the world a service by slaying any you come across."

Mekhi added, somewhat nonsequitorially that [[Asmodeus]] had a hit out on him.

Koh Tam let out a mechanical, unnatural laugh after a few moments, "Ha. Ha. Ha. That does not surprise me." He asked only that the party keep him in the loop about their exploits, "and if you are truly able to rescue Elturel, I may ask for your services in the future."

"I'll get back to you on that," Mekhi said, "can't speak for the rest of 'em."

"No matter. I will not hold you to it like a [[Devils|Devil]] might."
## On the River Styx
### Humphrey
Ilsa-vyx angrily, and unsuccessfully attempted to fish. Mekhi was notably thinner. Evryn heard a voice from one of his [[Soul Coin|Soul Coins]]. "Hello, I think it's time I introduce myself."

"Oh, uh, hello."

"Hello. My name is [[Humphrey]]. I'm sorry I've been so quiet, I've just been enjoying all the travel."

"That's fine, I supposed people had their reasons. My name is Evryn."

"Nice to meet you, Evryn. Do you care for a quick chat?"

"I wouldn't mind. How did you end up in a coin, friend?"

"Oh it's quite the story. I am an old, old man who loves travel..." he explained that on one journey he joined up with an adventuring party which thought they'd find their fortune in [[Baator|Hell]]. He warned Evryn about the [[Hellwasp Nest]], how the horrors there made everything else they'd seen were like nothing.

"We've fought [[Warlord Gangs|Warlords]] and their armies. What do you mean it's worse?"

"They have reason. In the Nest it's just bugs..." he wasn't sure what happened exactly, only that everyone died and that he abandoned them. He asked Evryn leave him behind in the nest. "My travelling days ought to be over."

Evryn asked what he actually wanted, rather than what he thought he deserved.

"Well... I guess it may seem obvious, but I just wanna keep travellin'. I've always believed you can find beauty in any place, even someplace as foul as this."
### Mekhi and Koh Tam
[[Koh Tam]] approached Mekhi, "I need you to tell me the truth about where your soul has gone."

"For a long time," he answered, "I thought it was right here... where it should be." He paused, "I don't know if you know [[Page, the Facilitator|Page]], but they *enlightened* me about the reality of the situation. So I figured I'd get another deal, few more options. It's all above my head now, but... Found myself at [[Bel's Forge]]. Didn't want to be working for a [[Demons|Demon]], so I figured it'd be better to throw in with a [[Devils|Devil]]. So I don't know if he's got it, or someone else's got it, or if it's in the ether. I haven't had it for a long time."

"There is an expression amongst your kind: *out of the frying pan, into the fire*. So I ask you: how are you going to get out of the fire?"

"I haven't given up, but..." he explained he figured he might find a way to get his soul back in all [[Baator|Hell]]'s paperwork.

"Your head is in the right place. You are between two masters. But I do see a path to your freedom; a narrow path."

## The Hellwasp Nest
![[Hellwasp Nest.png]]

[[Olanthius]] took [[The Meat Grinder]] around in order to make a distraction if the need arose, and quietly the party climbed up a chain.

Battle was inevitable. Evryn took a crowbar and freed a mace from a wall while Kiarin collected some of the sticky yellow sap from the ground, inferring that *it* was the divine essence they came for. Mekhi filled up an empty [[The Big Gulp|big gulp]] with the stuff and the party blasted a hole in the wall to make their escape. Unfortunately, during this, Ilsa-vyx fell unconscious and neared death.
## Levistus
Ilsa-vyx found herself in a cold room before [[Levistus]]'s throne. "I received your message. About [[Rinne]]. It seems we have much to discuss. But you are running out of time. I'll put it bluntly, Ilsa-vyx: you are dying. You've been so reluctant to receive any of my aid and it's broken my heart. But now I wonder, do you die along with your principles? Or do you finally allow yourself to receive the help I've been more than willing to provide. It doesn't have to be difficult, Ilsa-vyx, but I suppose it's in your hands."

Ilsa-vyx looked at her wounds and thought of recent events. Mekhi was out of there, but just barely. Evryn and Kiarin made it out safe. She was fading. "I spoke with [[Olanthius]] recently and Olanthius told me to get my troops in line. Which doesn't really make sense in my head, because it feels like... everyone is helming their own [[Infernal War Machine|War Machine]] down here, I guess. And we've carved this path through [[Baator|Hell]] together, and everyone's made a couple of deals to get by. Even me, I haven't made any deals with [[Devils]], but I still told Evryn that we'd do ok, and I still promised him that [[Celosia|his mom]] would be safe. I miss mine. I told Mekhi he wouldn't need to make any new deals, and that he'd just need to rely on us to make it out of [[Avernus]], to get his soul back. And... we were apart for a while so it's kind of hard to sear in the capacity, but Kiarin is a sister and some days it feels like she has more in common with a metal shield than with me, but that's ok. I think, if it came down to the wire, and if they were going to get out and be safe it wouldn't be with me taking the wrong end of a contract I can't say no to. You wouldn't keep pressing if there wasn't something valuable in me. Thieves don't break into empty homes. I won't do it. If it was for one of them... I don't know. But I feel like Elturel is in good hands no matter what happens. In spite of it all."

"And yet you won't see it. You won't see it saved. You would choose death--painful, imminent death--simply out of principle. You're making a mistake."

"It's my mistake to make. I look at Rinne and I look at the sibling that took the bad end of a deal, and I don't think it's my choice to reflect poorly. My uh... my [[Jade Strongwind|mom]]'s been gone for a while. I really miss her, and that was her choice. And part of mine was to go looking for her. Maybe this is another way to find her, but I won't take any shortcuts that you provide."

"Then you'll be seeing her shortly." The lair melted, "you will not escape so easily, Ilsa-vyx. The next time we meet, I will not be so comfortable."

"Me either. I'm gonna go find my mom."
## Back to the Barge
Ilsa-vyx let the poison out of her system, and Evryn translated the inscryption on the mace from [[Celestial]]: *"Wicked fiends, flee in terror."* Spending some time with it confirmed it was a **Mace of Disruption**. Kiarin left [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]] with Evryn for a bit, which was somewhat of a surprise to him, and left to speak with Mekhi.

The magical aura surrounding him--the usual necromancy latent to his being--was much weaker. He looked gaunt and tired. She avoided talking about something, instead asking him how he was doing.

Mekhi admitted that he wasn't doing well. He didn't know where his soul even was. "Maybe \[[[Bel]] and [[The Collector]]] are the *good* options. So I ain't doin' good, Kiarin. And I don't wanna say 'but I'll live', but... I don't know."

"... Do you need something? Water or... something?"

Mekhi stood up, "I need to know why you did it. Why you stopped me."

"I don't know. I guess I just acted, and it... it was all kind of a blur."

"Well we've still got a job to do. As long as we're still working, then... I don't know about Karnivash, but if it comes back to bite us... I don't know if I'll have the strength to deal with it, then. You're the smart one, Kiarin, you always were. You've got the schemes, the plans, all the smooth-talking. I hope that you'll be the one to figure out the right thing to do."

Kiarin promised she would see things through, and Mekhi rested.
### Meanwhile
Evryn spoke with [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]]. He asked him how he was, "you almost got thrown into a lake of fire and Mekhi doesn't trust you."

"I have been worried about Mekhi for some time."

"This is not about Mekhi, this is about you."

"Mekhi flung himself into a pit and forged a deal with a [[Devils|Devil]]."

"No, this is what I'm saying: I want to believe that you're not trying to pull a fast one on Kiarin or on the rest of us, but I have no clue. I've never been able to get a read on you. Tell me why I should believe you when you say you are not some entity with bad intentions."

"I see. I told this to Kiarin, but I truly have two desires. For one, I want to be freed from the shield and return to my normal life, but I also want to help you four free [[Elturel|your city]]. I have also tried to guide you toward the pieces of this [[Staff of Power]]. I believe it can destroy the artifact above your city, keeping it trapped here. What I am saying is that freeing me and freeing your city can be accomplished at the same time. I hope I've been clear enough about what I'm trying to do."

"The thing I just don't understand is where this Staff or Power really came from, and how it ended up in pieces in [[Baator|Hell]]."

"It is said that the Staff of Power once belonged to a powerful [[Demigod]] who was slain within this lair of Hell and whose staff was broken and scattered throughout the plane. In my studies of [[Avernus]], I learned of this story, and was immediately captured by this great tale. It has been my mission for a long time to recover the missing pieces of this staff. I think a tool of that stature can be used to change the world. Wouldn't you agree?"

"For me, magic is waves of sound--music. I don't suppose how magic different from that would work, so I suppose no? Is it just raw power or is it for a specific purpose?"

"You understand. It *is* raw power, but with raw power an adept mage can channel all manner of effects. It is like the fuel for one of these [[Infernal War Machine|War Machines]]. I simply hope you understand that I am trying to help you. I cannot explain the rationale behind your friend's sudden bursts, but I fear he is not well."

"Hypothetically, you are able to find a way out of the shield, the city is saved; do you intend to take the Staff of Power after this is over?"

"I suppose I haven't thought about it... I suppose I'd like to take it with me, after all I've spent much of my life pursuing it."

Evryn explained that the party was not entirely unconcerned about leaving the Staff with him.

"How about we come to some arrangement: I have not shared the location of the last piece of the Staff, for fear you will dispose of me once I do. If you swear not to dispose of me when I divulge the location of this fragment, I will feel much more assured of my own safety. And I know the party listens to you. I believe in the strength of your word. And word is magic for you. So is that a reasonable request to make?"

"Listen, Karnivash, if there's one thing I've learned in reading so many stories, it's if there's a story of an underdog who surpasses their limitations and reaches their goals, and I want to root for you for that. And I know you weren't responsible for what happened when you were a shield, so I'll take your word that you're going to help us find the Staff of Power, use it responsibly, and I'll do my best to make sure nothing bad happens to you. And that you're able to reap the fruits of your hard work."
### Then
Kiarin returned, picked up [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]]. Evryn said that he and the shield had a good talk, and asked how it went with her and Mekhi. Tellingly, she avoided the question.

[[Koh Tam]] told the party that they were fighting against the current, and that [[The River Styx]] had changed. They wouldn't be getting back to the [[Exiles' base]] anytime soon, but they'd be safe on the barge. "And try to get along with that [[Bearded Devil]]. He's... *slacking*. It is not uncommon for some [[Devils]] to remain on my barge to shirk their duties. He'll have a day of respite before I politely tell him to leave."

"What's his name?"

"[[Zor]]."

Afterwards, Ilsa-vyx and Kiarin fished (unsuccessfully). And [[Humphrey]] commented that Evryn didn't leave him behind, and that they recovered the mace. "Are you sure you want me to keep travelling with you?"

"Absolutely, if you want to."

He thanked Evryn, and the [[Soul Coin]] dissolved, and the weight of his story washed away.
## Perception
In the middle of her rest, Kiarin saw something strange. She saw [[Zor]] muttering into something; a glint of reflective glass in his hands. *Divination. Detect Thoughts.* The tail end of a conversation: "Yeah it's been taking forever to get to the Fortress. Ferryman's been taking forever. Plus he's got these idiots. A goblin, a drow, some royal knight looking guy, and- yeah they're still here. No I haven't talked to 'em. Ok, ok. I'll see you soon."

She cast an alarm over the room where he was and went to inform [[Koh Tam]]. He wasn't told a destination, but he had some realization. He ordered [[Tiax]] kill Zor, which he did gleefully. Everyone who wasn't already woke up. They quickly grabbed the magical shard in order to figure out a destination, in the hopes of figuring out just who was coming to get them.
