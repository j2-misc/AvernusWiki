# Verbose
The party drove through ash rain to [[Levistus's Emissary]], [[Rinne]] and [[Kara]] in tow. She spoke up, that her sister asked her to share something with the party: "I can tell you [[Levistus]]'s true name."

Kara explained that with Rinne's ability to speak with the dead, they learned Levistus's true name from a lost soul. Apparently, in their overconfidence, they tried to bind the [[Archdevil]] to their will. "To make a long, traumatic story short, simply having the true name of a [[Devils|Devil]] doesn't grant you total control of it. I ended up in a block of ice, and my sister spirited away to this place. I'm telling you this with a warning attached: you can leverage Levistus's true name, but if you do you'll make an enemy of him. Can I trust you four to be smart, and not get us all killed?"

Ilsa-vyx had [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]] removed from earshot, and Rinne spoke Levistus's name. The word slithered through the air. *Na'tas*.

Evryn asked Rinne what she would offer to Levistus and she clarified she had nothing, but she was hoping the party could do something. "I know I'm asking for a huge favor, but I'd rather be in your debt than his."
## The Emissary
They were ushered through the mountain range and up to the [[Magic Mirror]]. They stepped out of their [[Infernal War Machine|vehicle]] and spoke with [[Levistus]]. He commented that they took a while and asked Mekhi what he would do if someone were to wrong him.

"Has someone done that here?"

"Answer the question."

"... try to make even with them."

"Really? I didn't expect you to have such a gracious heart."

"I didn't talk about generosity."

"How would you describe it, then?"

"I told you. I'd get even with 'em."

"I see. Such amusing terms you folk come up with. But I would agree! I think if someone wrongs you, it is only right that you get even with them. [[Rinne]] has committed a particularly grievous offense with me, and thus her servitude is her way of 'getting even.'"

"What's your plan here?" Mekhi asked, "Maybe we can help each other out."

"I see Rinne has taken some rather distracting company. She's become distracted with her work. I'm not content with that. I imagine I will have to better supervise her."

"So you're saying if this distraction was rid of, we'd all be the better for it?"

"Certainly."

"I think we can do a bit better than that. Rinne's just one soul, right?" Mekhi offered a [[Soul Coin]] for Rinne's freedom, and a vial of divine essence for [[Kara]]'s.

"Even if I wanted to free that wretch, she is a soul. And a soul removed is a soul lost. You'd effectively be killing her."

Kara quietly assured the party that she was ready to pass on. She died during their first encounter with Levistus, and she was glad she had more time than most to make peace with her fate.

Ilsa-vyx questioned Levistus on an earlier comment, that the power to move one soul to another body was beyond him.

The details all seemed settled, save one. Levistus clarified "I require Ilsa-vyx to sign for the deal." He offered a parchment contract, even adding that he'd go so far as to ensure Rinne's safe passage to the material plane.

The party asked why, and he explained. "When in [[Baator|Hell]], you do as the [[Devils]] do. And time after time, Ilsa-vyx has refused to participate in the social contract. A less civilized devil would have vanquished her, but I consider myself civilized. Either way, Ilsa-vyx will learn that when you are in hell, you do as the Devils do." And then when pressed he gave some devilish answer that it was as honest as it appeared to be.

Ilsa-vyx made one final offer:
- A new body for Kara
- Both Rinne and Kara back to the material plane safely
- Safe passage through and throughout Levistus's realm

And in exchange:
- The party would not go around telling everyone Levistus's name, *Na'tas*.

"I see they told you." Levistus spoke as though grinding teeth. He offered a new contract:

> "Levistus shall`[, in exchange for payment in 1 (one) Soul Coin,]` liberate Kara’s soul from its captivity and nullify his pact with Rinne, whom he shall safely and without harm return to the Plane Material. In exchange for these services`[ and safe egress from Levistus's domain]`, the signers will grant Levistus permission to wipe their memories of any information pertaining to Levistus’ true identity."

Mekhi added in that they require safe egress from his domain, and Ilsa-vyx asked she additionally be granted information about [[Jade Strongwind|her mother]].

"For that information, I'll need something more."

"Oh will you, Na'tas?"

"Don't. Push your luck."

After some more consideration, such as a potential agreement later on for the information of the fate of her mother, Ilsa-vyx signed the contract.

Kara thanked the party and assured them that she'd owe them one on the other side, and gave Evryn permission to tell her story. Then she turned to Rinne and said, "Everything's gonna be ok."

Kara's coin dissolved into powder, and the soul was liberated. Rinne closed her palm over the dust. "I'm free... she's at rest... and I get to go home..." She looked to Ilsa-vyx, "I'm sorry. About all of this, and that you had to deal with Levistus. I'm so, so sorry."

"I hope you'll be wiser in the future. Or not. I'd be happy to employ you again in the future," Levistus mused. Then, the memories of the party were wiped.
## Elsewhere
Kiarin revealed she'd secretly written down [[Levistus]]'s true name, so the party still had it even after their memories had been wiped. They returned to the [[Exiles' base]] and shipped out.

The journey was slow, but thankfully uneventful. As they neared [[Elturel]], the [[Fire Giant|Fire Giants]] positioned themselves and started preparing a runic circle.

As they made their preparations, the party saw [[Demons]] approaching, the [[Sibriex]] they'd encountered previously leading the charge ([[Session 17, 13 November 2024 - A Duel with the Devil|Session 17]]). After a sickening melee, Ilsa-vyx managed to land a staggering blow with her Mace of Disruption, causing it to rupture into nothing but disgusting ichor.

After a few more rounds of combat, the portal was finished, everyone was ushered through, and they found themselves once more before [[The High Hall]].
## Elturel
Guards and [[Ramius Dangremond|Ramius]] offered aid. They were taken to priests to be healed. Kiarin whose condition was particularly bad, was carted off to a more secluded wing of the hall.
