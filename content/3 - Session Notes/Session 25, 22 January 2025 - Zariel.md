# Verbose
In his rest, Mekhi was visited by [[Bel]] once more in his forge. "It is time we talk about the details of our contract. I don't think you know the gravity of your situation. I found it quite difficult to pluck your soul from your [[The Collector|prior employer]]. In the meantime the power that I can offer you is limited. Your access to magic will not be what it was before, but I can offer you this..." Bel offered his hand; a smattering of runes, each representing a different element. "After you learn those runes, it is my hope that your work in the forge can compare to that of the four giants I lost."

Mekhi consigned himself to his fate. "Your will be done, Bel..."
## Good Morning
Mekhi awoke to the sound of a [[Bearded Devil]] being popped. [[Tiax]], who gleefully slew the thing, informed the party they were wanted on deck. [[Koh Tam]] seemed to be in a rush. He said that he could banish something powerful, but it would take time to prepare, so if some threat were to emerge, he asked they do what they could to stall it.

A ring of smoke surrounded the barge, and a woman emerged clad in crimson armor, a halo of flame above her bald head, and wings like an angel. [[Zariel]]. "I haven't introduced myself. You four are so hard to contact." Her eyes betrayed her hatred. "I will make this very simple," she said her, smile dropping, "why are you causing so much trouble."

Evryn spoke first, "we are trying to save [[Elturel]]."

"That city of sniveling fools?"

Ilsa-vyx chimed in, "We are trying to save our home!"

Mekhi added that their families lived there. Good people.

Zariel laughed, "trying to save an anthill. I'd've struck you down if you weren't so entertaining. But I am here to be civil, despite everything. After all, you have stolen something that belongs to me," she looked past the party toward [[Olanthius]]. "You four are more than welcome to traverse around my land, to your heart's content. How foolish! What makes you all so certain you can even save Elturel? Tell me."

Mekhi commented something along the lines of, "I'd take you a bit more seriously if I didn't know the stories. How you used to help Elturel. Time makes fools of us all, I guess."

And Ilsa-vyx added that they had the mandate of [[Torm]].

Zariel's face shifted, "heavensent by Torm. His four little toadies. That *god* has not set a foot into hell. That god has not lifted a finger to stop [[The Blood War]]! He would rather sit in his throne than aid as millions are slain!"

Mekhi noticed Koh Tam was casting a spell. The banishment. He took the opportunity to provoke Zariel. "You two seem real similar from our perspective, letting millions die."

"And what do you know, insolent orc? You lack my perspective to end the blood war myself. I came down here to do what the gods would not! You should be thanking me, mortal, for fighting on your behalf!"

"Is that what you're doing?" Evryn asked.

"It is."

"How does Elturel play into that?"

"Every army needs soldiers. Ten thousand souls of Elturel should do. *We* will make your material plane safe, that you can sleep soundly at night without fear of [[Demons]] knocking down your doors."

Mekhi said, "Well guess what, *lady*, me and my family are here because of you! We've seen the frontlines, and lemme tell you: those demons seem to be doing really well against your army!"

Zariel snapped back, and Ilsa-vyx asked her what this even was, "you're here because we're entertaining? Is this for the war effort? Or is this just toying with us to make you fucking feel better?!"

"I grow tired of this conversation. Step aside."

"Not without a fight," Mekhi said firmly.

Evryn asked, after some hesitation, "what makes Olanthius so special to you?"

"It doesn't matter. He swore an oath to me."

"The person he swore to died."

"You mortals live such short lives. I know things you could not even fathom. And I have seen the power of an oath."

Kiarin feebly attempted to stall her just a few seconds longer by placating to her, but she did not care for any conversation at this point.
## The Fight
She summoned a magical barrier, splitting the party, "It would have been entertaining to flounder about trying to save Elturel."

Zariel attacked the party as though swatting flies. When finally Ilsa-vyx drew blood, the corners of her lips curled just slightly. Ilsa-vyx saw past this, though. She could tell the [[Archdevil]]'s heart wasn't in it. Perhaps due to her comparative power, but perhaps...

"Prepared to strike down another [[Hellriders|Hellrider]]? Like so many years ago?!"

"Stop talking! And die already!"

Mekhi charged in, emboldened by Bel's runes and decked Zariel across the face. Mekhi's chains extended from his arm into a fiery restraint, binding her in place.

Kiarin tried again to distract Zariel with conversation (she wasn't interested) so she retreated to Olanthius's position, cast a ward of *protection from evil and good* upon him and told him to go help. He refused, causing her a great deal of hysteria.

Mekhi managed to shrug off one of her attacks and taunted her.

She cried with delight, "Worthy challengers!"

"Wish we could say the same for you!" Ilsa-vyx's voice boomed. She then laid into Zariel with attack after attack, "This one's for [[Jander]]! This one's for [[Celosia]]!"

"Take their names out of your mouth!"

"Takes one to know one, idiot!" *miss*.

"They might have been cowards, but they fought better than you!"

Finally freed from Mekhi's chains, Zariel flew over to the other side of the barge, where Kiarin, Olanthius, and Evryn had retreated. She declared, "Olanthius is mine!" then after a moment of looking around and finding no sign of the paladin, she asked, "where is he?"

Just moments before, Evryn had polymorphed Olanthius into a rat and quietly tucked him away in his pocket. Answering Zariel, Evryn said that he sent Olanthius away. She was going to kill him until Kiarin managed to stall her by suggesting that if she killed Evryn Olanthius would be gone for good.

From the other side of the barge, Mekhi taunted, "You running away just like [[Haruman]]? I killed that man on the floor! Guess he learned it from the best!"

Zariel tuned him out, but when Evryn asked to make a deal, she lowered her sword. Suddenly, golden light wreathed her and she cried out, "What are you trying to-!"

Koh Tam's spell finally went off, banishing Zariel.

For the first time, the party saw his hands. They were made of metal, crafted by a tinker's hands. "I must leave you by the shore, gain some distance by the time she returns."

"Try not to die, Koh Tam," Mekhi said.

"Should've never taken this job," Tiax cried.

## Exiles
The [[Fire Giant|Fire Giants]] quickly ushered the party in, and confidently boasted they'd crush any potential pursuers with a rock.

Evryn restored [[Olanthius]]'s form. He said he was uncomfortable being a rat, but it worked, "so thank you."

"I couldn't just let her take you."

"Why? Risk your life to save mine... why?"

Evryn seemed puzzled for a moment, "I... suppose it was that... if push came to shove, you didn't really have a choice in the matter if [[Zariel]] got to you, but that was... but that was a choice I did have. And I wanted to choose to protect you."

"Evryn, you are a man of great honor. I can see now why [[Torm]] sent you and your friends to rescue [[Elturel]]. From the bottom of my... absence... of a heart... nevertheless, thank you. You stood up to Zariel of all people and made it out alive. Surely the gods favor you."

"I appreciate it. I hope Torm's judgement was correct."

They parked their [[Infernal War Machine|War Machine]] and went to see [[Celosia]].

Kiarin took [[The Big Gulp]] of celestial essence and prepared to follow the ritual of Celosia that she'd memorized when she first went comatose. The party did whatever they could to help and waited.

Air returned to Celosia's body, the color returned to her skin, and she lurched from her bed.

Evryn embraced his mother. She asked if everything was ok, but he didn't respond. "I'm so sorry," Celosia said, "I'm so sorry."

[[Hellripper]] had everyone vacate, giving Evryn and his mother some time.

## Outside
[[Hellripper]] asked the party for help in getting [[The Hells' Exiles]] to [[Elturel]]. If the party could distract the legion of [[Demons]] beneath the city, the [[Fire Giant|Giants]] could use some magic to transport a large number of people. That plus assistance from [[Gargauth]] and [[Lulu]] might prove just enough.

[[Celosia]] emerged from her room, the party and Hellripper informed her about the plan. "Thank you, [[Hellripper|Gwendolyn]]."

Then, in a moment alone, Evryn spoke with the party, "We need to talk about [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]]. He and I talked for a moment on the boat and I feel that... I feel like we might have been a little... too... rough and judgmental on him, honestly." He looked to Mekhi, "I don't know exactly what prompted you to want to toss him in the [[Bel's Forge|forge]], but it doesn't feel like he's the bad guy in any of this."

Mekhi said, "That's what you think, huh?"

"That is what I think. Tell me why I should think something else."

"I ain't gonna say you are wrong," he wrung his hands against his ethereal chains. "I've had time to think. About [[The Hidden Lord]]... and maybe he's helped us, but it ain't about what I think. What I know is that my old boss... [[The Collector]]... wants him. And I know that he ain't a good guy. I think he's got a part to play in the shit we find ourselves in, a [[Demon Lord]] and all that. Powerful enough to make [[Bel]] second guess his own actions. So maybe you are right, that *thing*, or person, or whatever's inside that shield, but if The Collector wants him, then something's up. Even if he is a victim of his own circumstances, I need to get back at him. I need to get back at that bastard, make sure he pays the price for messing with me."

"So it sounds like for you it's not about Karnivash at all."

"I also don't like his attitude, but yeah. It ain't so much about him, so much as it is about what he is."

He looked at the rest of the party, "can any of us say we wouldn't have done what he did in the same circumstances? That he did anything we wouldn't?"

"Work for a [[Demons|Demon]]," Ilsa-vyx commented.

"You can't always control that," he said gesturing to Mekhi.

Ilsa-vyx remained obstinate, Mekhi added that Karnivash was playing a losing game and that he might bring them down with him.

Karnivash spoke up, asking for kindness as he shared that the last piece of the [[Staff of Power]] was hidden away in [[The War Slough]], one of the major fronts of [[The Blood War]]. There, the final piece laid at the bottom of a terrible lake.

Before the party split, Kiarin attempted to comfort Evryn, that Mekhi was stubborn but that she believed he wouldn't act against the party again.
## Rinne
No one else wanted to talk to [[Rinne]], so that responsibility fell upon Evryn. She was impatient, perhaps justifiably, but the party was justifiably late. Evryn said he'd talk to the party and see if they could escort them to [[Levistus's Emissary]] in the morning, but Rinne and [[Kara]] insisted they leave immediately. "[[Levistus]] mentioned not that long ago that I'd better be at his Emissary, or else. I don't wanna find out what 'or else' means."

Evryn returned to the party and told them about the situation. The party was hesitant, especially given Ilsa-vyx's situation with Levistus, so they decided first to question Kara and Rinne under a *zone of truth*.

The party asked if they would betray the party given the opportunity. They were silent for a moment, but said they likely would. Rinne also said she was bound to obey Levistus's orders. Mekhi took the opportunity then to make a pact with the sisters under the effect of the spell, and they agreed not to betray the party in exchange for their help.

While the spell was active, Ilsa-vyx asked Evryn what deal he made with Karnivash and he said that it was just a casual deal that he'd not let anything bad happen to him if he told them where the last fragment of the [[Staff of Power]] was. Nothing like a pact or anything. Ilsa-vyx then asked Karnivash what he planned to do once he got the staff. He said he'd like to return to his studies. "Is that all?"

He fell silent.

Kiarin spoke with him telepathically, and asked him privately the same question, and if that was all. He said he'd like to return to his studies, speak with some colleagues, practice some new spells.

*"I suppose I'll have to find out."*

*"Shouldn't you be questioning them?"*

*"I'm talking to you now."*

*"I don't think we have time."*

*"Evryn has magic to spare."*

*"Levistus is impatient."*

*"I don't care about Levistus."*
