The party returned to the bad barrel where they'd left [[Kupu]] but found that he'd disappeared somehow. They discussed their options then, arguing somewhat about where to go next; either [[The War Slough]] or [[Ninth City Advocacy Services]].

Ilsa-vyx suggested first, though, that they try and establish some communication with the [[Celestial]] trapped in [[The Companion]], while she and Mekhi check in on [[Ali]], with whom he seemed to have a rather *tense* history with.
## The Companion
Evryn gave Kiarin his [[Demon Signet Ring]] and the two flew up toward [[The Companion]]. Evryn lagged behind while Kiarin went closer toward the storm in an attempt to make telepathic contact with the [[Celestial]] trapped within the infernal device. They were in unfathomable agony, and consequently didn't have much useful to say, besides that they were dying. While the last fragment of the [[Staff of Power]] likely wasn't going anywhere, the Celestial might die before they could find it.
## The Docks
Mekhi and Ilsa-vyx went to the [[Dragoneye Docks]]. A [[Hellriders|Hellrider]] warned them against venturing further but they assured them they'd be fine. They pressed on toward what appeared like a war zone. Passing by an alleyway, both noticed two men dressed in crafter's clothing trailing them, as if about to strike. "You two can come out," Mekhi said, "we're here to talk."

The men complied. The dwarf asked, "who are you?"

"We're here to talk with [[Ali]]."

The men led Mekhi and Ilsa-vyx further into the docks. Soon they heard the sounds of battle, a skirmish between [[Ali]] and her bandits and a two-headed [[Demons|Demon]]. Mekhi simply watched, though Ilsa-vyx ran in to help. Ali smiled at her after the whole thing was over with, and both looked back to Mekhi who seemed rather displeased.

"I thought you'd be better than this, trying to carve out the city," Mekhi commented.

"Not trying to carve out the city. Just watch over my own," Ali said.

"Well, I'm glad you're being *very selfless* in protecting your people, but I'm not here to talk about that. Just here to make sure you're not causing any more trouble for the city than as is."

"You got me wrong. I'm here to protect the docks, and I don't trust the Hellriders to do that. They never cared *before* the fall, so I don't see why they would *after*. Especially given they're all busy in [[The High Hall]] anyways."

Ilsa-vyx, when dragged into the argument, suggested (on-record) that conflict was bad and that alliances were preferable to conflict and asked (off-record) if Ali could help the party steal something from [[Ninth City Advocacy Services]].

There was an instant shift in her demeanor. "Well, well, well... good cop becomes the bad cop! Tell me more."

Ilsa-vyx kicked Mekhi in the shin, indicating it was his turn to talk. "Everyone in Elturel--especially you, by the way--has their soul tied up in a contract binding them here. So our plan--which I wasn't planning on telling you--is to steal the contract so no one here is damned for all eternity. We're here just to make sure you play nice while we're out doing that."

"So what is it exactly you want from me? Tools, supplies, anything that could help. But most importantly, your word that you won't do anything to let this city fall into any more peril while we're gone."

In turn, Ali asked what he and the party would do with anything particularly valuable. She suggested they give that money to the people, adding, "if you funnel that over to the Hellriders, I'll kick your skull in."

Ilsa-vyx asked, "What do you all need the money for?"

"Assuming we ever get out of this place, we're gonna need to repair this place. You think the Hellriders are going to help the docks first?"

Ilsa-vyx empathized with the concern, but expressed that everyone in Elturel was suffering, and so everyone needed to be uplifted.

Mekhi added that, after the heist, the party would worry about the bigger problems while Ali could count coins.

Ali said she wasn't willing to part with anything she needed, but offered a magical scroll (dispel magic).

"Thanks."
"You got it, Boss!"
"She's not the boss!"
"It's a term of endearment from Waterdeep."

"And Mekhi," Ali added, "remember my money."

## The Heist
### The Plan
The plan was simple: go in disguised as [[Devils]], insist on seeing the security measures for themselves due to concerns over a [[Lazivos|change in leadership]], see the upper vaults to deposit a contract, jump the guards, steal everything, and leave before anyone is any the wiser.

Evryn cast a *seeming* spell over the party, disguising them as Devils (Mekhi, [[Chain Devil]]; Ilsa-vyx, [[Merregons|Merregon]]; Evryn, head honcho [[Incubus]]; Kiarin, [[Spined Devil]]) in black suits working for a nameless boss (who they could name as [[Bel]] if need be). They stepped inside.
### Entrance
The corridor ended at a pair of double doors with a motto inscribed in Infernal: *"Better the devil you know."*

They approached the (rather rude) receptionist and signed their names when asked. Evryn felt as though something had been taken from them. Kiarin telepathically suggested they steal the book on the way out. Inside were a number of tables where people sat and ate pastries and were occasionally led to private rooms. A [[Cambion]] woman stood behind a desk, over which hung a portrait of another Cambion with dark gray, almost metallic wings wearing ostentatious clothing, a mote of flame between his horns.

![[Lazivos.png]]

They were stopped on the way over to one of the doors, and spoke with the receptionist. She asked them to wait for a consultant. Ilsa-vyx overheard someone complaining to their familiar, that [[Akistos]] would never have made them wait this long. Evryn could sense the energy in the area was that of the DMV. Eventually [[Thrubwell]], a pudgy [[Devils|Devil]] with a brass nameplate and thinned hair, spoke with them.

He led them to a back office and scratched at his strange necklace. Evryn said that the party was sent to scout out the bank and ensure that the change in management had not in any way affected the quality or prestige of [[Ninth City Advocacy Services|Ninth City]]'s vaults.

Thrubwell's eye twitched, clearly having dealt with this question a lot, and he gave a standard diplomatic answer.

Evryn asked for a more palpable assurance of quality: a tour of the upper vaults.

"While I understand your employer- by the way who is your employer?"

"I'm afraid that is on a need to know basis, and we're not at a point where you need to know. Rest assured, we'll make sure your time spent is worth it."

"Right... but it is unfortunately not Ninth City's policy to allow non-staff to tour the vaults."

He was then presented with a bribe, and Thrubwell, choosing his words carefully, offered to *invite* the party to speak with some of the guards. "Surely," Evryn said, "we can speak with the guards who frequent the Upper Vaults?" Mekhi flashed a [[Soul Coin]] as well, a sign of more to come.

"Yes- yes, I'm sure you'll be satisfied by the quality of security of our establishment. Our... Head Guard can assure you that Ninth City is better than ever, I assure you. In the meantime, I'd love to see that Soul Coin..."

"And you will. In time," Evryn said as the consultant snagged the coin pouch (100 gp).
### Guards
[[Thrubwell]] led the party to [[Aemilios]], the head guard, introducing them. "Well, Thrubwell, I assumed you'd know by now that it is against policy to allow non-staff into our vaults for any reason."

"I know that, but I believe our clients here represent a rather *affluent* benefactor! I'll leave the rest to you," he said to the party, "and I'll be sitting at my office if you feel so inclined to leave your compliments."

Emilios's guards were similar to the tall [[Merregons]] they saw in the reception area. He set his pen down and asked, "so what are you all really here for?"

Mekhi spoke up, "you speak common?"

Emilios, in common, answered that he did, somewhat.

"We don't want your employers overhearing our conversation."

"You think you're the only one to try and bribe your way to the vaults?"

"No, but we're gonna give you the best offer," Mekhi slid a [[Soul Coin]] over.

"Thought this was reserved for Thrubwell."

"Think we've only got the one?"

"Who are you working for that's got you flaunting out Soul Coins so freely?"

"Our boss is looking to make some moves, potentially for some new lucrative- *future* assets," he flashed the symbol of [[Bel]] emblazoned on his skin.

"Well I'm sure you're aware Bel is no longer the reigning [[Archdevil]], and our chancellor is not necessarily aligned with his interests."

Mekhi and Evryn intimidatingly suggested that he ought to hedge his bets rather than wait and see how [[The Blood War]] shakes out. He seemed somewhat skeptical, but changed his tune slightly.

"You bring up some reasonable concerns... but I must ask: why are you seeking to enter the vaults directly? Standard procedure is to leave contracts with consultants like Thrubwell."

"Do you trust people like Thrubwell with your security?"

"I see your point." With the bribe of a Soul Coin, he'd personally deposit the contract for them. First though, he'd have to shake Evryn's hand, per Bel's (Mekhi's) request. He cast *charm monster* and asked that Emilios escort the party to the upper vaults despite the policy. Despite the guard's better judgement, his personal greed and ambition took over. He'd go along with it so long as the party refrained from disclosing anything they saw within the vault. They agreed, and walked down the stairs to the Upper Vaults.
### The Upper Vaults
They walked into an office space, one of the rooms appearing to be a sort of holding cell. They heard (and later saw) [[Tiax]] bound by gold shackles inlaid with a sapphire. He shouted angrily something about employment. Apparently, he'd attempted to steal some contracts, "as if he could bypass our security so easily."

"May we have a word with him, actually? [[Bel]] might be willing to take him off your hands."

[[Aemilios]] shrugged, "Spares us the decision of what to do with him."

They approached Tiax and he saw through their disguises once they were close enough. He played up the act and he and Kiarin telepathically communicated, explaining the situation. He wanted in, and apparently had his own form of telepathy in addition to truesight ([[Celestial]]?). Evryn then stunned the guard and the party quickly went about robbing him and freeing Tiax. Kiarin took the necklace which acted as a keycard and went about unshackling the strange gnome, meanwhile Mekhi found a gilded finger necklace (150 gp value) as well as a whip which transformed into a dark purple feather when he touched it ([[Quaal's Feather Token, Whip]]).

Quickly then, after Mekhi stole even the clothes off the guard's back, Evryn shackled him and Mekhi gagged him. They shoved him into the prison and the guard screamed, but they figured that since they couldn't even hear Tiax until they approached, it would be fine. Mekhi tossed the clothes they stole to Tiax who commented, "these ain't gnome sized, y'know."

"Get bigger, buddy," Mekhi joked.

Tiax then commented that he should probably be going.

"Like hell you are," Evryn retorted. "You'll blow our cover! You'll get out in a bit, but first you have to help us." Eventually, he relented.

They pressed on to the next room, where a guard sat with his eyes closed. Ilsa-vyx and Kiarin voltroned into a tall [[Merregons|Merregon]] guard trench coat situation and entered.

![[Akistos the Curators Business Card.png]]

He presented the card to Ilsa-vyx and asked her opinion, "new business card, whaddaya think?" Ilsa-vyx threw around a bit of nice business lingo, complimenting the card. After a strange interaction, Ilsa-vyx explained they were there to retrieve a contract on behalf of [[Bel]]. Then, he asked another question just before retrieving it, "what kind of contract is it?"

Ilsa-vyx turned the question back into a compliment of previous leadership.

"Is it written in Levistole or Mephistopholes Plus?" *He was asking about fonts.*

"A custom font. Belvetica."

"Hold on now... that's not an approved font. Has he sent in a font approval form?"

Ilsa-vyx, mind racing, "this is confidential information, you cannot share any of this. Do you understand?"

"I do."

"There is a new management shift not just in the pinnacle of the hierarchy, but soon in the places where business was once done well, business will be done well once again, as it was in the good old days."

[[Akistos]] seemed excited at that prospect. "Is that right? What does that mean for someone like me, then?"

"We will be able to discuss in due time, *High Vaultkeeper*."

"Actually, *Senior* Vaultkeeper. You see at the moment, that [[Lazivos]] the *Infernal Chancellor* (not even a real job) has stolen *my* position as *Senior* Vaultkeeper. I mean, how much experience can he have? Over me? What do you think?"

"Little Lazivos... it is not in my station to speak." She went on to try and hurry him along by emphasizing the lack of efficiency of the modern system under current leadership, and eventually, a spirit exited the obsidian pillar behind Akistos.

Mekhi tried to sneak in to get the contract, but was spotted. Instead of responding as one might expect, Akistos turned up the volume on the music he was playing in the background and asked Mekhi what he thought about the music.

"It's a bit gouache." A scene legally distinct from American Psycho briefly played out, and Akistos grew suspicious.

Quickly, Ilsa-vyx explained that Mekhi was a new Lazivos hire, screening security. Akistos didn't seem to believe the lie, but instead of drawing a weapon, he remained calm. "I can abide by disguises and such, but ignorance of fonts... distaste for beautiful music... I must know why you need this contract."

"To showcase a failure in management," Ilsa-vyx responded quickly.

"You're right! Tricking the counselors, getting past the guards... yes all that shows I'm not suited for *Junior* leadership."

Kiarin sensed that he'd been onto them from the beginning, but he saw in them an opportunity. She suggested that if the party were to steal not just one contract on his floor, but a far more important one in the Lower Vaults as well. Akistos then set down Mekhi's contract as well as a *scroll of teleportation* when his music box suddenly failed. He was about to flip out when suddenly Evryn replicated the music to keep Akistos calm.

Thankfully, they managed to head away from the Upper Vaults and down toward [[Zariel]]'s contract on the floor just below.
