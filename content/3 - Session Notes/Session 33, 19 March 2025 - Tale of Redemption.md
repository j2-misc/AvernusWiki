# Verbose
With a magic pen, they drafted a contract with the intent of getting [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]] to sign (33 Intelligence (Sleight of Hand)).

Outside [[Elturel]], Evryn summoned a pegasus, [[Endurance]]. [[Demons]] had taken defensive positions around the descending city, awaiting impending [[Devils|Devilish]] assault. To get past, they needed a distraction. So they made a martyr of [[The Meat Grinder]], sending it charging into a horde of Demons rigged to explode.

[[Gargauth]] met them as they landed and informed them that things in the city were not well. When asked, they told him that [[Koh Tam]] was well. [[Ramius Dangremond|High Rider Ramius]] and [[Zythan Avhoste|Zythan]] met them in [[The High Hall]], along with the yet-living former generals of [[Zariel]]--[[Lulu]], [[Olanthius]], and [[Jander]]. Evryn raised [[The Sword of Zariel]] aloft for all to see.

"Elturel's liberation is nigh!" Ilsa-vyx declared. Then, they informed the party of their intent to redeem Zariel, and to kill Yashio.

After some deliberation, the party decided the best way to get to Zariel would be to simply turn themselves in. They recruited Jander and Lulu to this end, hoping they might help. They intentionally left Olanthius behind.
## Zariel
[[Zariel]] was on the front lines. They approached, hesitantly. She encircled them all with a wall of fire, a shield from the ongoing battle. "And what good do you intend to do, coming here and greeting me in the midst of battle?"

"We're here to talk," Mekhi said.

"To talk... you? Fugitives, traitors, Are here to talk?"

"We're here for two reasons," Evryn started, "to give you a gift, and secondly to make a request of you."

"In what position are you to make a request of me?"

Evryn drew [[The Sword of Zariel]].

"What do you intend to do with that old relic? Slay me with my own blade?"

"This is our gift to you."

"A gift," Zariel said almost indignantly. "What is the meaning of this? Why do you bring me my old blade," she looked to [[Jander]] and [[Lulu]] as they landed nearby, "and my old companions? What are you plotting?"

"There ain't no scheme," Mekhi said.

Lulu chimed in, "They mean it! They don't want to hurt you! They just want you to be nice and friendly again! Don't you remember? It has some of your niceness and goodness in it! Maybe it can make you good again :3"

Mekhi added, "[[Lady Yael]] wanted you to have it."

"You think you are trying to do something good here? What do you gain from this?"

"What does any creature gain from doing good?" Ilsa-vyx asked. "A path away from the life you live now. Instead of the source of [[Olanthius]]'s agony you can be a savior once more."

"Foolishness! I shall turn the tide of [[The Blood War]], save you all from the horde of [[Demons]]! Who are you to say I have strayed from the path of righteousness?"

"As of now you have more in common with [[Asmodeus]]."

"What do you know of [[Asmodeus]]?"

"Well he killed us ([[Session 18, 20 November 2024 - The Crypt|Session 18]]). Then talked with us ([[Session 32, 12 March 2025 - Heart's Blade|Session 32]]). In that order."

Evryn added, "We saw how you felt about him back in the day."

"I saw cowards! Those unwilling to act! Why don't you see it my way? Why don't you see the responsibility I've shouldered?"

"Because you're also a coward," Ilsa-vyx stated. "Because you're down here. This is the easy way. Maybe sometimes deals have to be made, but you don't have to stick by them, obey their rules, do their bidding. The hard way is more rewarding. It's worth fighting for."

Jander stepped forward to speak. "My Lady, Zariel. I know a monster when I see one. A monster has an insatiable hunger that can never be staunched no matter how many lives it claims. These people showed me that I am not a monster. How to replace one hunger for another; blood, for goodness. I want to see your hunger once more, Lady Zariel."

Evryn spoke of what he saw in her, a well-meaning spirit who saw no other choice in her pursuit of righteousness. To help people, like the villagers of [[Elturel]]. "That's not who you are anymore, but I feel this is a Zariel who can come back."

...

"Might I... hold the sword?"

A familiar scene.

*"I know you came here to kill Demons. You think you have failed. I disagree. Which of us do you think sees more clearly? Stand with me, Zariel."*

Unlike the prior vision, Zariel was in her devilish form. She looked back at the party. They said nothing. Evryn played a single chord on his harp, the starting chord of the [[Zariel|Tale of the Avenger]].

"You seek only to abuse my doubt, exploit my weakness instead of raising me to greater heights... you pervert all form of righteousness with your lies! No longer shall I heed them!"

She slashed through the vision, an angel once more.

![[Angelic Zariel.png]]

"What now?" Mekhi asked.

"Now we save Elturel," Evryn answered.

Zariel apologized to the party and informed them that with the chains shattered by the attack, and all her contracts destroyed, she could free the [[Celestial]] in [[The Companion]] and Elturel would return to the material plane. As they flew up to Elturel, Zariel seemed flushed with emotions. Ilsa-vyx gave her a pamphlet.
## Final Preparations
They'd remain in [[Avernus]] while the rest of Elturel was returned to the surface. So they could kill [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]] and [[Bel]], and with Zariel's help, should all go right, they'd be back home in a matter of hours.

Mekhi said his goodbyes to his friends and family. Ilsa-vyx gave word to the barkeep at [[The Dancing Horse]], and to [[Ramius Dangremond|Ramius]] who thanked her for all she'd done, promising to herself that she'd be back for [[Levistus]]. Evryn told [[Celosia]] about what happened and what would happen, omitting specifics. Celosia insisted that she'd stay with him, but he reminded her that she could not, that she was dying by being in [[Baator|Hell]]. He added that the [[The Hells' Exiles|Exiles]] would stay with her, and she relented and agreed to leave. "When you're on the surface again, go find [[Evryn's father|Dad]]. That way you can both be at my first retelling of everything that happened."

The party reunited, [[Gargauth]] in tow. The party huddled and Kiarin recalled that Gargauth might be an enemy of [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]], given that he prevented her from dispelling the enchantment on him, possibly restoring his true intelligence. Meanwhile, the old [[Hellriders]]--[[Lulu]], [[Olanthius]], [[Jander]], and [[Zariel]]--had a huddle of their own, stating that they'd stay as well. Handing a warhorn to Mekhi, Olanthius instructed him to signal when their help was needed during the fight.

Then, Zariel ascended to [[The Companion]], shed a tear, and uttered a celestial phrase, and the hateful aura surrounding it disappeared along with the prison, freeing the [[Celestial]] within.
