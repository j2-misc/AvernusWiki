# Verbose
They were face to face with [[The Cyst]]. It had a powerful divine energy emanating from it. The area around was completely barren and quiet, save the hum of [[The Meat Grinder]]. Ilsa-vyx prodded the exterior, leaving an indent in the crystalline-yet-fleshy material.

Inside there was a chapel and on its door was a celestial inscription,
> *Yield to the wisdom of the judge of judgements, the evaluator of values, the Silver Gauntlet [[Torm]].*
## 1
Evryn opened the door and they were met with [[Page, the Facilitator]]. *"I shall be the first of your three guides. Do you know why you are here?"*

Hesitantly, Mekhi answered, "... yes...?"

*"Then you know the eyes of divines, high and low, watch thee. Many watch you take your steps with bated breath, Torm amongst them."*

Ilsa-vyx asked, "Where do we go from here?"

Page beckoned the party forth toward the shrine. Then they lifted their arm and all began to levitate up toward the high rise. *"An angel soon arrives, questioning my lord about the problem of evil. Her seeds of doubt have not yet grown into an orchard."*

"What's the problem with evil?"
"It's evil-"
"I know that it's just-"
"ontologically."
"So is it-"
*"All shall be revealed. A judge of judgements, evaluator of values, my lord is both. In his role what might you decree?"*
"So we have to weigh in or?"
*"When asked, you answer."*

Ilsa-vyx whispered to the rest of the party that it was like when they were with Page the first time, when they rescued [[Olanthius]].

An angel stepped into the chapel, blue hair, a white cloth covering her eyes. [[Zariel]] approached the shrine, apprehensively. She had not yet fallen. She stared through the party, unable to see as she petitioned her god. "Great father, Silver Gauntlet. I first ask this of you, my lord, why do you remain so passive when the forces of evil spread across the world? This [[The Blood War|War of Blood]]... you are mighty, you are powerful, you have the authority to defeat these evils but why do you hesitate? Answer me and I shall abide by your words."

Evryn asked if the chapel had always been in [[Avernus]], and Page explained they were in the realm of the heavens in a moment long, long ago.

"Like a memory?"
*"Correct. You are at the crossroads of a memory and a vision. Speak wisely. She yearns for an answer."*

The party was somewhat confused by this task and deliberated until Page explained, finally, that Torm wished for them to see how Zariel fell, and to know the Zariel who once was so they might understand the Zariel they know now.

Ilsa-vyx asked, "Well what did Torm say?"

*"Torm maintained passivity. As you said, my lord had a greater plan. A greater vision beyond Zariel's understanding. Evil cannot be vanquished as easily as Zariel presumes. Although I explained this to you, I wish for you still to speak from your own hearts, to judge judgements, to evaluate values, to respond to Zariel whether you agree or disagree with the decision of my lord."*

Ilsa-vyx and Kiarin got to talking about thesis and antithesis and whether one could exist without the other through the metaphor of physical light and- Mekhi cut the explanation short when Evryn suggested he might have something to say. He posited that Torm must have been unable to do so, or that in doing so he'd create more evil, because those were the only sensible reasons why he couldn't interfere. They locked in their answer.

Zariel refuted this somewhat, by stating that she and his other creations were made by him, and she asked what, then, her duties were. Then, the party asked Zariel for what purpose she was created.

"I was created as an instrument of your will, to do good across the realms. But I feel as though I have not been utilized. I have done good when and where I can, but the Blood War grows and grows and threatens to spill into the material plane and harm the innocent. I feel as though I ought to do more." Despite her best effort, her face betrayed her discomfort. "I shall abide by the tenets of the Silver Gauntlet."

Kiarin briefly asked Page about the origin of [[Baator|Hell]] and [[Demons]]. The answer was the simple ubiquitous desire for power and sovereignty, by [[Asmodeus]], and the Demons.
## 2
Page brought them then to their next guide, ominously mentioning something about the end of their saga which created much existential dread, but they were cut off by the arrival of [[Koh Tam]] and his barge.

The party was happy to see him alive. "I knew you'd hold out, Koh Tam," Mekhi said, "you're built different."

*"Built is an apt description,"* he said showing off his metallic arms.

Evryn asked, "We all thought we saw you dead. Are you okay?"

*"I was slain, but I am a complicated being. I will do what I can to explain. But first, might I explain what my director wishes for you to see?"* He continued, *"Millennia ago, [[Asmodeus]] stood trial for his crimes, and the [[God of Order, Primus]], my lord, oversaw. Asmodeus argued his crimes were necessary to hold back the tide of [[Demons]]. Primus's ruling you will see now."*

Mekhi asked, "What's the deal with everyone's attention on us? There's more than just Torm?"
*"Yes. Your exploits throughout [[Avernus]] have made you in the eyes of many the heroes of a grand chronicle. I suppose my director wants to show you what is to come to equip you with knowledge, truth, a valuable weapon that you may wield in the days to come."*

"Were you made like that? Or did you turn into what you are?"
*"I am a being made to fulfill a particular purpose, which you shall soon learn of. From the moment I saw you four walk into [[Fort Knucklebones]], I foresaw you would make great changes and I am glad to have been proven right. I only hope that by the end of your journey order will be restored."*

They saw a vision of Asmodeus delivering his final address to the court of Primus. He explained how his realm stood between that of Demons and prevented their flow into the rest of the realms, that the Angels present were not victims, and that he was the only one saving them all from damnation.

Primus sided with Asmodeus, with two conditions:
1. Asmodeus was bound by the power of the Ruby Rod to ensure all his contracts were upheld
2. Koh Tam be deployed as a facilitator with diplomatic immunity

[[Zariel]] leapt from the stands toward Asmodeus, crying out against him and his campaign of evil. Primus intervened, housing Zariel in a cube of force. She declared then that she would descend to the Prime Material and stamp out any evil that rears its head, calling to the like-minded to join her in her crusade.

The vision faded and Evryn made the observation that Zariel and Asmodeus are not unalike now. Koh Tam agreed, adding on that they were both always driven by pride and ambition.

Evryn said, "I don't think Zariel was wrong, but I don't think it was very helpful in the long-run."

Koh Tam explained how he saw Zariel as a tragic figure, an example of how righteous means become wretched ends. *"Maybe that's why you're here. To change things."*

When asked, Koh Tam was able to offer little advice regarding the fate of Avernus, who should take rule of it. There was no clean answer.

Evryn asked, "Is there anything to be gained if we made no true successor clear?"

*"For there to be a vacuum of power in Avernus, Zariel, [[Bel]], and [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]] would need to be removed from their positions. Then, the future of [[The Blood War]] would be greatly uncertain, but I imagine that multiple powers, perhaps even non-fiendish parties, would all be interested in filling this vacuum."*

The party deliberated until Koh Tam interrupted, saying that he had great hope the party would make great change, but that he'd had that same hope in several groups in the past, and that many were consumed by ambition. Then he shepherded them to their third guide, warning them that he would say whatever he could to push them in the direction he desired.
## 3
The old battle was before them, [[Asmodeus]] stood before [[Zariel]]. He told her that despite what she thought of him, he did make a change and that he would win [[The Blood War]]. "Years ago at that trial, you thought I mocked you? No. I saw myself in you, walking the same path as I. You know as well as I do that as long as this continues, as long as the dead are nothing but tallies in ledgers... What I offer you is simple: the chance to continue your crusade. You said you'd not sit, that you'd stand. Stand with me, Zariel. Stand with me and we can end The Blood War." Then, he turned to the party, *"I have no need for middlemen to guide you. I wish to speak to The War Machines, the Heroes of Avernus themselves. Welcome. When I first saw you, I considered you to be little more than flashes in a pan. War Machines, you are the convenient solution to my ever-growing problem."* He gestured to Zariel, *"What will you do with her?"*

Ilsa-vyx answered, "Kinda what you did, I guess."

Mekhi added on, "See if she can become more reasonable."

*"Is that what you've been told? That there is still some shred of goodness in her heart? Approach her and remind her of the goodness deep down in her soul and she will shun her wicked ways and return to the path of righteousness. That's your plan?"*

"You have a better suggestion?"

*"I do. Taking Zariel's life serves the greater good. Whether in my service or that of the [[Celestial|Celestials]], she is a threat to herself and any who follow her. You need only ask the [[Hellriders]]."*

"You're saying Zariel is the problem?"

*"Exactly. It was Zariel who seized your city. It was Zariel who led the Hellriders to their deaths."*

"Then why did you offer her a deal?"

*"Then, I offered her a deal because I believed her zeal would make her a perfect general against the [[Demons|Demonic]] threat, but since then she has proved to be nothing more than a constant string of disappointments. The needle has shifted very little since her ascension.*

*"You've seen her incompetence, unable to stop even you. This serves us both. If you are willing to work with me, then I will grant you a boon. No contract. But you must promise me this: Bel lives."*

"Cute way of phrasing it, but that's just another contract," Mekhi said. "I'm not promising you that."

*"It is strange to have watched you all travel through the [[Baator|Hells]] and reject every advantage offered to you for the sake of these principles you hold. You are the opposite of pragmatic. Nevertheless, you are free to make your own choices,"* the phrase dripping with derision.

Zariel declared her fealty to Asmodeus. "I had such enmity towards you... Now I see you are only doing what I sought to do. I, Zariel, shall pledge myself to your cause and win The Blood War for your sake." She arose as the [[Archdevil]] the party was familiar with.
## Excalibur
They awoke to see a spectral [[Lady Yael]] holding [[The Sword of Zariel]].

![[Zariel's Sword.png]]

The party deliberated on what to do, Kiarin mentioned her hesitance to be involved in decisions regarding magical artifacts anymore, and the party hyped her up. Evryn then stepped forward to talk with Lady Yael. She had been guarding the sword since it was entrusted to her by [[Zariel]], unable to pass on before her duty was fulfilled. Evryn informed her about the fate of the other [[Hellriders]], how most of Zariel's generals yet lived. And she offered the sword to Evryn.

"Thank you for guarding the sword for so long, I'll make sure it finds its owner."

"Thank you... and please... tell my story... that though I have passed... I wish them nothing but happiness... Even Zariel... who confined me to this state..."

"Everyone will know of the burden you've bore."

Lady Yael passed with a smile, and the sword, the blade of Zariel's heart, spoke. The blade had a power that each of the party could activate, but only at the right time.

[[The Cyst]] opened for their egress.
