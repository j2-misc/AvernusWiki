# Verbose
[[Lord Yashio]] held the [[Staff of Power]] in hand, animated shield orbiting him. "Thank you, truly, for freeing me." He was the same [[Demons|Demon]] they saw at the [[Idyllglen|the Battle of Idyllglen]] ([[Session 6, 7 August 2024 - Idyllglen|Session 6]]).

Mekhi steeled himself and asked Yashio, "Are you, um... what's that behind you?"

Yashio cast a wall of force around himself, the party drew weapons, and he spoke, "before you attack, allow me to explain myself."

"Talk fast," Evryn commanded. Meanwhile, Mekhi slashed pointlessly at the barrier.

"I am Yashio, but you may continue to call me the Hidden Lord. It is true that I've manipulated you into freeing me from my captivity, but I intend to keep my promise to you four. My intent is to destroy [[The Companion]] with the staff as I told you I would. Before I do, there is one obstacle that we must overcome together. Once [[Zariel]] is removed from her position, [[Bel]] will take her spot. I don't believe any of you want this. Given what he has done in the past, I believe he will oppose us as we try and rescue [[Elturel]]. If we continue to work together to defeat Bel, then we have a clear path out for Elturel."

Evryn asked, "You want to help us defeat Zariel and Bel?"

"With Zariel, you seem to be on the way to find [[The Sword of Zariel|her sword]]. It possesses great power. As for Bel, you will have the support of the demons I'll rally."

Mekhi chimed in, "So you want us to help you win [[The Blood War]] for the [[Demons]]?"

Yashio confirmed and invited Evryn to cast zone of truth. He asked Mekhi to calm. "I have something to offer you as well. The name Karnivash that I used is not my own, but that of [[The Collector]]. It could be just what you need to get out of his service."

"So making another deal will get me out of this situation?"

"I've given it to you for free." He asked the party why they were so quick to judge.

Yashio and Kiarin had a dialogue, through which she realized that Yashio was responsible for razing Idyllglenn, under the instruction of [[The Collector|Fraz-Urb'luu]], the real Karnivash, but it was him who set into motion the events that led to Zariel's fall and the damnation of Elturel.

Mekhi asked Yashio how they could guarantee he'd stick to his word, suggesting a contract, which he resisted. Then, he offered an ultimatum: when they'd return to Elturel, they would see Bel and him. They could slay Bel and he would destroy the Companion, freeing Elturel, or they could wrench the Staff of Power from his cold, dead hands. Then he was gone.

"We need to move," Ilsa-vyx said and started up the car.
## On the Road
They discussed how they'd get into [[The Wandering Emporium]]. Evryn suggested he could cast some magic to disguise them as he had at the [[Ninth City Advocacy Services]] ([[Session 28, 12 February 2025 - Avernus Psycho|Session 28]]).

Mekhi assured the party they would kill [[Lord Yashio|Yashio]].

Evryn asked, "Is it bad that I'm not immediately opposed to his offer?"

"Yes." Ilsa-vyx stated as a matter-of-fact.

Mekhi argued that it'd end [[The Blood War]], that [[Demons]] would run wild through the planes. Evryn suggested the Blood War wouldn't end, but they would continue to fight the [[Devils]]. "They'd just have the upper-hand."

"Yashio isn't going to keep a close eye on every Demon, and he even said himself he'd only be content *for now*. Whoever moves in is gonna be nobody good, but I don't know if we can be making those sorts of decisions for everybody."

"He wasn't lying when he said he had no intention of going into the Material Plane."

"Yeah. He didn't." Mekhi clarified, "I'm not saying we don't work with him. He's powerful. We take advantage of him, make him sign a bad deal. He believed me, I was lying--sorry about that, Evryn."

"I knew you weren't affected by the spell."

"Well thank you for not telling him. But we have him help us kill [[Bel]], then once he's weak we pounce on him. Hells, maybe we get him trapped in another shield if we're lucky." There was a long moment of silence. "What do you guys think? Ilsa-vyx? I know you're not a fan of working with fiends, but I think we get everything we need out of it and give him no quarter, y'know."

Ilsa-vyx didn't respond immediately, as they pulled up to the Emporium.

"Kiarin, you're smart, maybe you've got some ideas for the contract?"

Kiarin didn't respond.

Mekhi turned his attention back to Evryn, "we can't trust this guy."

Evryn related the situation to his experience with the [[Soul Coin|Soul Coins]]. "Even if there are Demons going through here rather than Devils, people are still going to be hurt by it. This way it's just more in your face. Isn't that what the [[Hellriders]] are more equipped for? Guarding an entrance?"

Ilsa-vyx demanded, "Explain what you mean by that."

"Devils work from afar, with people, slowly worming themselves into affairs on the Material Plane. Demons for the most part don't do anything like that. If they're going to attack someone they go out and attack someone. One is a lot easier to spot and resolve than another."

"Evryn, you can undo deals. We've undone 'em, we'll undo some more. You can't undo a demon," Mekhi said.

"And you would compare Hellriders to demons? Did Ilsa-vyx hear that correctly?"

"I'm just saying the Hellriders as they are right now are more in their element battling an enemy they can see."

"Evryn, you just got tricked, bamboozled, fooled, you're rolling over, aren't you mad? Aren't you going to do something about it?" Ilsa-vyx asked.

"I'm upset. We were lied to, manipulated. Pitted against one another-"

"You were given a sob-story-" Mekhi interjected.

"Let him talk!" Ilsa-vyx commanded.

"I just wonder what's going to hurt people more. And the old status quo of Bel sucked. That's the reason Zariel came down in the first place, but we have a chance to pick between these two different evils. I'm just entertaining the idea that maybe the guy we hate more is actually the better option."

"Or," Mekhi said, "we go with none of them. We free Zariel, we kill Bel, *and* we kill Yashio, and let [[Baator|The Nine Hells]] sort out who's going to be the new boss so we can get our people out safely while Hell reasserts maybe a better status quo, maybe a worse one, but not one that's going to be dictated by people we know are going to fuck us over." After a pause he added, "And we'll kill [[The Collector]] too, but that's a long term plan."

"Realistically, if we kill all these people then it sounds like this Collector guy will be next in line."

"There'll be a fight for the power vacuum, it won't just be settled. At least then we're not just giving the win to either side. We'll be letting them figure out who's the better one. Either way we're dealing with the consequences on the Prime Material no matter what, so we'll let them sort out their own down here."
## The Wandering Emporium
The crowd was thinner. Far fewer [[Devils]] than typical, shopkeepers more bored and anxious than last time. Evryn disguised them as nobodies.

Mekhi attempted to catch Kiarin's attention by talking about potentially stealing from shops to better help them gear up, but it didn't work. Ilsa-vyx stepped out to get a head start on looking for supplies.

Evryn and Mekhi went to a merchant to sell off some valuables they'd stolen from [[Aurumvas]] some time ago ([[Session 19, 27 November 2024 - Union Dues|Session 19]]). He made up some stories about the goods, Mekhi chiming in as a "neutral" interested observer. They managed to talk him into buying 250 gp worth of goods for 460 gold.

Then, Evryn brought Kiarin along to [[Burney's Barber Shop]] while Ilsa-vyx doubled back to lock down the car and Mekhi oversaw some basic supplies. The shop was unoccupied. Save for [[Burney]], "You're back! How've things been?"

"A lot. Some good, some bad. We were able to help [[Soul-trapped Wyrmling|Tirzah]] out."

"What happened to them? Did you free their soul?"

"They found peace. And... we did a *little* bit more than just that," and he explained the whole interaction with [[Kupu]].

"I gotta ask though," Burney spoke quietly to Evryn, "what's wrong with her?" referencing Kiarin, who was still sulking.

"Dealing with some stuff."

"Seems like she has a lot on her mind. Let me see if I can help her out," and Burney offered to cut Kiarin's hair, and Evryn was thanked again and excused. "Thank you again, for helping with Tirzah. They were a good one."

Burney tried to cheer Kiarin up by telling her a story. Afterwards, and with a fresh haircut, she left, passed by Evryn playing a familiar song, and broke into [[The Infernal Rapture]] to make herself a grilled cheese sandwich (burned).

Mekhi then sought out a potion seller, finding a merchant peddling ichors of fiendish origin. He purchased a powerful [[Demons|Demonic]] ichor (roll 2d6, polymorph into a Demon of that CR; side-effects unknown). After, he met back up with Evryn. It was at that time they realized they were not aware of where Kiarin was. Mekhi looked for signs where she might've gone, and Evryn went to tell Ilsa-vyx. She responded as one might when informed that the weather had turned.

"Mekhi went to look for her."

"Yes," Evryn confirmed.

"Thought so. If you want to go look as well, Ilsa-vyx will be here."

Mekhi and Evryn found, pretty quickly, found that she was at The Infernal Rapture. They broke in, closed the door behind them, and found her sulking, head down at a table. Mekhi eased into a conversation and commiserated with her that it wasn't easy to get over being backstabbed. "There are no right steps to getting over that, I mean obviously I wouldn't know, look at me. I've... what I've made two deals with an otherplanar entity, maybe now a third. Maybe not the best, but I think you can be... well, I know you're a lot smarter than me, but uh... we can get back at him, you know, we can get even. You don't have to go throuhg this trouble because of the fuckups that they did. It sucks that he's out of the shield now, but that wasn't all you making those decisions, so it ain't your fault at all."

Evryn tried to cheer her up as well, offered her a hug, and she cried for a while. After a while, they started leaving, Kiarin stole all the silverware (made of tin, for Devils), and Mekhi started a kitchen fire, bribing the sole janitor to leave and not say anything.
