# Verbose
[[Zariel]] advised the party to be cautious in their confrontations with [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]] and [[Bel]]; that they were cowards and would fight like it.

Yashio telepathically contacted Kiarin, asking her why she'd left, but she confirmed that she hadn't and that she and the party still intended to help him slay Bel. He awaited them at the corpse of a three-horned pit fiend. They offered him a contract in the Waterdhavian (lie) tradition.

After some negotiation, they had the following terms:

- The first line of this contract is rendered null and void should the party make any harmful act against Yashio \[tee-hee :3\]
- Yashio cannot attack or harm the party directly or indirectly (such as by proxy)
- Yashio will assist in the endeavor to depose Bel to the best of his ability
- The party cannot attack or harm Yashio until such a time as Bel is defeated
- Yashio must forfeit all present and future claims to realms outside Avernus
- Yashio must forfeit all present and future claims to the Tower of Urm (non-negotiable)
- The party forfeits all claims to all realms within Avernus (save the Tower of Urm if it happens to reside temporarily within the Hells)
- All signing parties must, to the best of their ability, protect and ensure the safekeeping of the contract
- The terms of this contract should end at such a time the party is returned to the Prime Material plane

Impatiently, Yashio signed and he added that if there was any sort of clause that put him in harm's way, that it would be a part of [[The Collector|Fraz-Urb'luu]]'s plan, for the [[Staff of Power]] to fall back into his hands. For it was Fraz-Urb'luu's plan for Yashio to fall in [[Idyllglen|the Battle of Idyllglen]], to set in motion Zariel's fall. Then he informed them of his plan to destroy Bel. He would lead his troops to draw out the (former) [[Archdevil]]'s, while the party could infiltrate his tower perhaps by flight. "By the time he is weakened, I will have seized the tower and will destroy him with a magical barrage."

The path ahead was clear. Evryn imparted with them some final words of wisdom, for the party's last hurrah.

They managed to fly up about four-hundred feet to the top. The gunners trained on them, and Kiarin teleported Mekhi into a [[Winged War Machine]] at the tower's peak. The key was on the dash. It was likely a getaway vehicle, Mekhi's now. The rest of the party continued their ascent, Evryn confusing their assailants by casting light off their position to draw fire away from them, Ilsa-vyx tossing the odd [[Devils|Devil]] tumbling to the ground below. They collected in the [[Infernal War Machine|War Machine]], drove off the edge, circled around, and flew into the window.
## Inside
[[Bel]] himself sat in his hovering throne, occupied by some sort of telepathic communication spell, anger and panic flashing across his face in equal measure. Mekhi floored the vehicle toward him, ramming it into the former [[Archdevil]], while the rest of the party bailed. A fight broke out, "What are you trying to do, Mekhi?"

"What does it look like? I'm trying to kill you!"

Meanwhile, Evryn approached a nearby teleportation circle astride [[Endurance]]. "You don't have the guts to activate me," the circle cried. After some intimidation, he managed to convince the thing to summon [[Zariel]] and her generals. Mekhi managed to whip Bel around into the scythes on the front of the war machine while the cavalry poured in. They were all just in time to witness Ilsa-vyx lay into Bel with her mace of disruption, and witnesses would attest in the years after they saw [[Torm]]'s gauntlet on her hand that day.

Then, [[The Hidden Lord|Yashio]] and [[Shummrath]] arrived, and Mekhi declared the party's intent to slay the both of them. Yashio asked why the change of heart.

> "She is going to sheathe her sword, and she is going to- if she can, in this moment- approach Yashio at a normal distance. Broom of Flying to be level with him. And she is going to say to him as earnestly as she can manage, extend her hand out towards him, 'If you hand over the staff now, this doesn't have to turn to violence.'" - June/Kiarin. 

> "Why must this turn to violence at all? What do you have to return to? A mother who pities you? A broken home? Performances for copper at dilapidated inns?" - Yashio.

> "I'm not sure, but maybe if you turn over the staff- we can find out together."

"I do trust you, Kiarin. I promise you this: If you remain here and rule alongside me, you will have all the power and adventure you could ever ask for. And as a sign of my trust, bear this," and he handed her the [[Staff of Power]] and dignified, she took it and returned to the party, and gave the order to attack the [[Demons]].

Mekhi and Ilsa-vyx dove headlong into combat with Shummrath. Yashio issued a command to Kiarin, "Nullify your contract at once." Inoculated against his charms, she resisted.

Evryn then cast a spell, targeting Shummrath. He commanded, "*Bathael!* Do not resist!" and polymorphed the [[Demon Lord]] into a toad. He quickly then picked him up and tucked him away. Yashio commanded him to throw him onto the ground, but he was too weak to fully shatter the spell. Kiarin then managed to steal attunement of the Staff and teleport the toad away.

Then, Yashio dispelled the magic on [[Gargauth]]. "Let's get you back on my side..."

More coherent than normal, his countenance shifted and Gargauth asked, "... why am I here...?"

Mekhi called out, "Yashio set you up! He set all of us up!"

"I... know you... you fought with me... why?"

"Because you're our friend!"

"Don't listen to these mortals. We are Demons."

"Gargauth, you can be more than whatever cosmological phenomena that made you wanted you to be!"

"Mekhi, Gargauth is a tool. Made by [[The Collector|Fraz-Urb'luu]] and discarded. Now he is a tool for me. Do what you were made to, Gargauth."

Then, [[Gorgonash]] charged in, said some pleasantries, then declared his unchangeable intent to devour the party's souls. Meanwhile, Kiarin infused all of her magic over the course of a few turns into retooling the Staff of Power into a magical nuke. With Ilsa-vyx's aid, she was able to take hold of the unfathomable magical power. For a moment, she could've directed it in any way she *wished*.

*"This didn't have to happen. I could've ruled this kingdom, you could've gone home, and we're now shedding each other's blood endlessly. If I could wish now, I would wish nothing but torment for each and every one of-"*

*A beam fired from the staff to Yashio's chest, and another struck Gorgonash. As more Demons arrived more magic projected toward them, slaying them.*

*"What... what is happening...?"*

*Ichor bubbled to the surface and amalgamated on the Demons' skins.*

*"I don't know what's happening, but we must leave," Zariel said urgently.*

*Mekhi shook Kiarin and she was half-responsive. Gargauth asked to go with the party, and Mekhi said it wasn't even a question.*

*"What... did you do... to the Staff...?" Yashio asked*

*Kiarin lazily tossed the staff to him and it shattered as Zariel teleported everyone safely away.*
## Home
The air was cool, fresh. The earth was soft beneath them. There was green grass, and a sun to set, giving way to the night's chill. At last, they were home.
