# Verbose
The party ducked into a side office to hide from passing guards, assassinating an [[Imp]] at the same time to keep everything quiet. They then passed down the stairs toward the Lower Vaults, allowing the two guards to live and likely encounter and free [[Aemilios]].
## Downstairs
There was an aura of Abjuration and Conjuration, quickly *dispeled*. The party used the emerald green keycard on the door and pressed inside. There sat a well-dressed [[Devils|Devil]], [[Lazivos]], and behind him were paintings...

*Ilsa-vyx* asleep at a bar, a tankard spilled on a table. A reflection within it depicted the party taking [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]], Mekhi signing his contract, numerous depictions of Evryn speaking with Levistus. Your fault. Your fault. Your fault.

A painting of a crowd, of a group of dockworkers, like in [[Elturel]]. None stood out, but there was a face like *Mekhi's*. Not what he is, but what he was, what he did to avoid his fate. Thin, haggard, covered in soot and sweat. All worked tirelessly, Mekhi chained to this life like every other nobody.

A family portrait devoid of family populated only by an otherwise unremarkable depiction of *Kiarin's* [[Octavia Uriss|mother]].

*Celosia's* room, the party rushing in with the nectar, performing the same sort of spell, but far too late.

The Devil was relaxed, as though waiting. He clapped his hands modestly. "Well met."

"What is all of this?" Evryn asked.

"A test, you could call it. I hoped you'd be able to reach me so I could speak to you. Please do step forward and take a seat."

"You knew we were here?"

"I knew you were here from the moment you signed your names onto the paper. I must commend you for getting past most of my faculty. I'll have to do some hirings and firings, but your wits in that regard did not hinder my knowledge of your coming. Not to put you at fault, of course. I've ensured not *much* trickery can happen here. So please, sit. We have an offer to discuss."

"That's a nice offer, but first you gotta look behind you there's an ugly bitch staring at you," Mekhi pointed at the painting Lazivos had of himself. "Seriously though, do you have any reason we should be talking instead of beating you up and robbing you blind?"

"Because I have an offer you might like to accept. [[Zariel]] is a good friend of mine. I tell her things and she tells me things. I have heard tales of your resilience, and your strength, and your goodness. But I think you're fighting for the wrong side. Zariel thinks so, too. And there is much you have to gain from changing your employment."

The party was extremely resistant to this, as Zariel had damned them all to [[Baator|Hell]]. Lazivos offered ascension to something more righteous--soldiers in the blood war--to them and all of the souls of Elturel. Mekhi continued to argue against this until Kiarin cut him off, identifying the offer as a distraction, to stall for time until reinforcements arrive. Lazivos denied this, but the party didn't much care for whatever he could offer them anyways.
## Lazivos
Ilsa-vyx teleported behind him and attempted to strike him, but he charmed her with his mystical words, preventing her from attacking. She redirected her blade at Evryn's painting, the rest of which imposed their truly rancid vibe upon everyone else.

[[Lazivos]]'s goons arrived shortly thereafter and started attacking the party as they fought back against him. "These [[Devils]] are just a few elevated beyond their station as glorious soldiers against the [[Demons|Demonic]] threat-"

Kiarin quickly pointed out they weren't being used against Demons at all at the moment and suggested he could go fuck himself. Ilsa-vyx, no longer charmed, destroyed two paintings, leaving only hers and Lazivos's left. Incensed by Lazivos's words screamed at the Devils, "TRAITORS! TRAITORS TO ELTUREL TRAITORS TO THE MATERIAL PLANE! ILSA-VYX WILL NOT LET YOU CONTRACT HER FRIENDS!" her wild rage surged beyond her, flooding the entire room with a barbarous spirit.

Lazivos struggled to continue arguing as Mekhi dragged him across the floor, "You don't know what you're doing! You don't know what it means to be a leader! [[Zariel]] is the only leader here!"

Ilsa-vyx was knocked out, brought back, got huge, and charged Lazivos, taunting him as she bloodied him, "we've had to make deals with [[Levistus]] down here. You're not him. And Ilsa-vyx is gonna level with you here: [[Akistos]] is going to take your job. You're gonna die in this room. And for what? Mekhi, hit him again. War is the natural state of things, and peace only happens when people work really hard. And let me tell you: these four people, working really *really* hard. I'm gonna get so much [[Hellriders|Hellrider]] overtime for this," she mused as she slammed into him again. Lazivos managed to escape, threatened [[Tiax]] into fleeing.

Kiarin, meanwhile, teleported into a crawlspace which led into a sterile vault, wherein she found a number of items in an open chest (into the bag of holding) and a locked chest (easily unlocked) which contained just so many solid gold bars. She stashed the treasure, found the contract labeled under "[[Thavius Kreeg|Kreeg]]".

Lazivos struck Ilsa-vyx down, sending her tumbling to the ground, bleeding out.

> **

Lazivos said through pained groans, "I have pledged-... to serve Zariel-... all through my life and even-... beyond! Even if you kill me-... it is already too late! Your best bet is- is to-... join-... her! Do not be-... a fool-...!"

"Alright, sure, fuck it, show me the contract."

"You finally-... see reason!" For all his zeal, Lazivos was a coward, easily deceived if he thought it meant he could keep his life. "Join me!"

"Oh yeah, right, but I'm gonna need to see wording. Some good hard paperwork to sign." As soon as Lazivos turned around, Mekhi swung on him. He barely dodged out of the way.

"Why? I don't understand?"

It really didn't matter. Mekhi said, "I'm going to kill you, Lazivos. It's gonna make me happy."
## Levistus
"I will let you kill him," he said to Ilsa-vyx in her unconscious state, "nothing in return. Only that you will kill him."

"No... fine print? No... contract? No... handshake?"

"You have become impatient with my deals, I have become impatient with your stubbornness. Yet we still need each other."

"Ilsa-vyx has always been impatient with your deals."

"Right."

"How about a why? Is there a why?"

"I think you already know what I'm after. And I will get it one way or the other, just as I was able to get it from your mother."

Ilsa-vyx stated that she trusted her friends even though they made mistakes. "And when historians look back at this moment, they will know one [[Hellriders|Hellrider]] said no to a contract. Eat my ass."
## Thavius
Meanwhile, Kiarin collected the rest of the contracts, stopping only to examine one of the contracts Zariel held with Lazivos. It essentially read:

> Be it known that I, Thavius Kreeg, foresake my name and title to take up the role of Infernal Chancellor Lazivos

She relayed this information to the rest of the party. Ilsa-vyx then looked over at him and saw not Lazivos the Devil, but the haggard old man whose head she'd caved in some weeks ago.

"I signed that contract fifty years ago to save Elturel! Not to damn it! Not then and not now! Now the city shall be reborn! Its people uplifted! Granted true holiness! Why don't you see this? Why don't you see that what I've done is not just for the good of the city, but for the entire world!"

Evryn asked simply, "How does this improve the world?"

"If you realize the truth of my words and pledge yourselves to Zariel, you'll see She is the truth! She is the one who will win [[The Blood War]]! Saving the multiverse from the endless assault of [[Demons]]! It is for the greater good!"

"Honestly I feel bad for you," Mekhi commented, "put all your money on the wrong horse."

The party gathered together and put the coward out of its misery.

Mekhi looted a key and a ring from his corpse. Ilsa-vyx offered a prayer to [[Torm]] over [[Thavius Kreeg]]'s corpse, and took his head. Then smoke formed around them, the telltale sign of Zariel. She appeared with sword ablaze while the party fumbled with the scroll, managing to teleport to the lobby. They shocked the secretary, stole the ledger, and booked it toward [[The Meat Grinder]]. They tossed the contracts into the machine, destroying them as they drove as fast as they could away from [[Ninth City Advocacy Services|Ninth City]].
