# Verbose
[[Ramius Dangremond|Ramius]] informed the party that a gang had sprung up in the [[Dragoneye Docks]], preventing aid from entering the area, under the control of [[Ali]], who Mekhi seemed to have some history with. In addition, rocks sometimes fell from the sky, [[Demons|Demonic]] patrols had grown more frequent, and [[Zythan Avhoste|Zythan]] had made a new discovery. He suggested seeking him out when they had the opportunity.

After the party dispersed, Ramius spoke with Ilsa-vyx about the [[Hellriders#The Old Hellriders|Old Hellriders]]. She said she hadn't gotten to known much about them, save [[Olanthius]] and [[Jander]]. She explained some of their history. Ramius decided to offer her (temporarily) a sword passed down through the Dangremond line on the condition that she wield it as a leader. "A sword can cut, but it can also inspire. If you understand this, then you're worthy to take up this blade for the sake of [[Elturel]]."

She accepted the [[Marshal Shortsword]].
## Zythan
Kiarin showed up a bit early and spoke with [[Zythan Avhoste|Zythan]]. She asked something about if he could divine intent, but he said he couldn't, laughing it off a little. She noticed in his notebook, he was writing about possibly making a deal with a [[Devils|Devil]], [[Kupu]], though she couldn't surmise the immediate details. She stole the book for later examination.

The rest of the party arrived and asked about his recent discovery, that [[The Companion]] drew from a confined [[Celestial]] within it, and he suspected that if it was freed it might cease to function. He said he figured it out by some divination of his, but Evryn noticed that he seemed a bit shiftier than normal, like he was giving himself a bit more credit than he deserved.

Regardless, Zythan went on to say that the storm clouds surrounding the Companion would likely strike down any who got close. He said he'd speak more with one of his contacts later in order to see if he could discern anything more. He asked about the [[Staff of Power]] and the party confirmed they'd found a final lead, but Mekhi had reservations, and thought it might be a better idea to first seek out [[The Sword of Zariel]].

Mekhi then took the opportunity to ask Zythan about [[Ninth City Advocacy Services]].

"Are you planning to make a visit there soon?"

"More like a break-in, but yeah."

"Is there anything you need to know about Ninth City?"

"Anything you've got on it."

Apparently, the [[Avernus]] division of Ninth City was recently under new management, [[Lazivos]]. He suggested that being a new appointee, his unfamiliarity was likely a point of weakness. "I don't know much about its defenses or other staff, but any and all of your wit will be of great use there. You may have to lie, and cheat, but that is the way of the [[Baator|Hells]]. You might be able to free the souls of [[Elturel]]'s citizens that way."

The party dispersed, Ilsa-vyx asked if Kiarin and Zythan were like... entangled...? She said she wasn't, but that the reason Zythan was being weird was that he was consorting with Devils, and that she'd confront him about it later. She assured Ilsa-vyx that he wasn't a concern and that he was a good person and that the whole thing was likely not much cause for concern. Ilsa-vyx raised that she had a tendency to be biased in favor of wizards, like Zythan and [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]], speculating it might have to do with [[Octavia Uriss|her mother]]. Kiarin then quickly, and awkwardly left.
## Elsewhere
Ilsa-vyx and Mekhi ran through some sparring exercises, taunting each other even before they started.
## Mekhi
Mekhi went to check in on [[Gargauth]], and he was incredibly happy as he could kick [[Devils|Dee-vils]] and nasty [[Demons]] (only the nasty ones (Gargauth was a good Demon :3)). Gargauth asked about [[Koh Tam]].

Mekhi lied and said that he was well, knowing full well he was likely slain by [[Zariel]] ([[Session 25, 22 January 2025 - Zariel|Session 25]]). He said they'd find Koh Tam again and give Zariel a good kicking.
## Evryn
Evryn spoke with [[Celosia]], informing her about [[The Companion]]. He'd later meet with Ilsa-vyx at [[The Dancing Horse]]. In the meantime, though, [[Soul-trapped Wyrmling|Tirzah]] spoke up.

Tirzah said he'd been learning common, despite how difficult it was, and that he'd rather speak draconic but that he could manage. He figured that then was a good time to see if the party could free him from the [[Soul Coin]] he was trapped in. Evryn cautioned him that they couldn't restore him back to the way he was, and he sighed but seemed content.

Tirzah made idle chatter, asking Evryn his favorite color, his favorite food, and the meaning of life. Evryn figured the meaning of life was to live through interesting stories, which seemed to satisfy the [[Wyrmling]].

Evryn turned a question of his own back at Tirzah, "What is the best weather?"
## Kiarin
She read through [[Zythan Avhoste|Zythan]]'s journal, finding that [[Kupu]] worked for [[Bel]] and they'd been trading back and forth for information about [[The Companion]] and [[Ninth City Advocacy Services]]. She decided to seek out the rest of the party.

However, on the way over... she suddenly felt a telepathic link with [[Octavia Uriss]], her mother. "Kiarin Wyllow Uriss, where are you?! Why haven't you answered any of my *sending*s?!" Kiarin immediately cast *dispel magic* on herself, ending the effect, but the link was quickly reestablished. Octavia, even more irate, reiterated the amount of work she had to go through just to make this call; that she had to speak with [[Nancy]], and that she hated speaking with Nancy cause Nancy always goes on and on and on about her kids and this and that and blah blah blah. Kiarin attempted to dodge what questions were asked of her, further upsetting her mother. After a certain point instead of growing more angry, she sounded more dejected. She asked when she was coming home, and said that things weren't great right now.

"... You're still in [[Waterdeep]]?"

"Yeah."

Kiarin thought for a moment, considering if she should tell her that she was in [[Elturel]]. She ruled against it. If she told her mother the truth, it would only serve to worry her more, and that wouldn't help anyone. She figured she'd just try and calm her a bit by promising to stage a show there soon. "I'll... see if I can set up a show in Elturel soon."

"Elturel?"

"I- I meant Waterdeep, I misspoke-" but the damage had already been done.

There was a moment of silence, a loud crash, and Octavia asked, incredibly tensely, "Are. You in. Elturel?"

"... no...?" Perhaps her worst lie.

"You thought. It was. A good. Idea. To not tell me. Where you've been. All this time?"

Increasingly quietly, Kiarin responded, "Well- I know that sometimes you have a tendency to sort of... y'know, and I didn't want... y'know..."

Octavia got a little y'know and yelled at her daughter, "If your father finds out, I'm dead!"

"Maybe just... don't... tell him...?"

"Don't tell- don't tell! Wh- why didn't I think of that?! Oh I can just NOT TELL him oh how-! Not to mention- and- another thing! And I- and! That's it. I'm going to come find you. I can... figure something out."

"No it's- we've... we're fine we're working on some... stuff... down here."

"No it's-"

"*Kshhh*- oh I think we're- the signal- *kshhhh!*"

"Don't try that, that's not how this work-"

And Kiarin hung up. She then quickly met with the rest of the party.
## Kupu
The party reunited, Kiarin told them what she found in [[Zythan Avhoste|Zythan]]'s journal, and suggested they convince him not to go through with it, and Mekhi joked about Kiarin's romantic involvement with Zythan which she again denied. They all went over to Zythan's office, opened the locked door, and stepped in to confront him.

Ilsa-vyx posted up next to Zythan, and Mekhi acted as the bad cop to Kiarin's overly sympathetic cop. After some pressing, Zythan had [[Kupu]] reveal himself. With some convincing, he fessed up that Zythan wanted to make a deal: a year of service in order to find out more about [[The Companion]].

"You're really going to do this?" Ilsa-vyx asked.

"I don't have any other choice," said Zythan.

"There's always another choice."

Evryn came to a realization: Kupu worked for Bel, and made deals on his behalf, but they were currently speaking to *Kupu*. Meaning, that if they made a deal that appealed to his interests, they could cut [[Bel]] out. Tirzah quietly added that Kupu was very susceptible to being buttered up. The party decided then to trick Kupu by writing a contract in a language he didn't speak, and just convincing him it was a really good deal.

Kupu, fully convinced by the party and Zythan, asked they add the clause that he forevermore be known as Kupu, Scion of Bel. Kiarin, per instruction from Ilsa-vyx and Evryn, wrote in that not just his name but his true name should forever more be "[[Kupu|Scion of Smell]]". As he signed the contract, the words shifted from elvish to infernal and he discovered he'd been duped.

"What? Is there something wrong, Kupu, Scion of Smell?"

He immediately did a little dance, against his will, cursing the party. This delighted [[Soul-trapped Wyrmling|Tirzah]].

Evryn asked, "you got a message you wanna pass on?"

Tirzah considered for a few minutes, and simply asked Evryn to say something mean to him. He did, which caused a great deal of ego damage. The [[Imp]] attempted to flee, but Ilsa-vyx caught him, and the party tied him up and threw him in a barrel.

Zythan looked very down. He apologized for the whole situation, but thanked the party. "I'm going to entrust you with the responsibility of saving the city. I think I've done all I can." Then he gave Kiarin a string, one of his divinations (nat 1 portent).
## Outside
Kiarin suggested that Evryn take care of [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]] from then on. He asked if there was anything she wanted to talk about, but said she didn't. Karnivash then asked Kiarin why. They had a brief conversation wherein Kiarin conveyed that she thought Karnivash was right not to trust her and that he'd be better served by Evryn.

"When Mekhi was going to throw you in that magma ([[Session 23, 8 January 2025 - Hellfire|Session 23]])... I was certain I wasn't going to do anything. I felt sick for days afterwards."

"But you did save me. I do trust you. We just have a little bit further to go now. We'll leave [[Elturel]] soon and head straight for [[The War Slough]]. Once we have the final fragment of the [[Staff of Power]] there will be no more secrets. All will be revealed."

After a bit more coaxing, Karnivash convinced Kiarin to remain firm. "You're right, I'm- I'm being weird. I'm sorry," she went on to apologize to Evryn as well before splitting off from the party to spend time with The Hidden Lord.
## Tirzah
[[Soul-trapped Wyrmling|Tirzah]] spoke with Evryn, and thanked him for pranking [[Kupu]]. They said that they'd been thinking about what Evryn said the meaning of life was, and that even despite being scared to pass on to the other side, they would feel better if Evryn and his friends kept on adventuring. "Maybe I'll become a cool ghost dragon!"

"If you do, haunt me, it'd be really cool."

"I will!" and Tirzah soon passed, but he would live on through his story.
