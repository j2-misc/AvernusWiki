# Verbose
The party drove to [[The War Slough]] when all of a sudden everyone received a telepathic message, a birds-eye view of their own [[Infernal War Machine|War Machine]]. They heard [[Zariel]]'s voice accused them of turning [[The Blood War]] in the favor of the [[Demons]], placing a very public bounty on their heads. Whoever succeeded in delivering them to Zariel would be promoted to a [[Pit Fiend]].

Undeterred, the party made it to their destination.
## The War Slough
[[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]] guided them toward the battlefield. Then they saw the gilded wagon of [[The Golden Doom]] with [[Mahadi]], hands still bound by Sovereign Glue, and [[Feonor]]. More were behind them. It was a race.

![[War Slough.png]]

The party raced ahead, Golden Doom chucking skeletons at them the whole time. With some difficulty, the party managed to get to the final section: a lake of ichor where a strange humanoid figure began to wake. Kiarin hopped out of the vehicle, took the final fragment of the [[Staff of Power]] (the head, crystaline), and flew away on her broom to meet back up with the party as they'd circle around. With the fully in-tact Staff, Mekhi made a sick ass jump and they all escaped, watching as their competition was devoured far behind them.
## Karnivash
Kiarin was going to free [[The Hidden Lord|Karnivash]]. Ilsa-vyx and Mekhi cautioned against being too hasty, but Kiarin seemed resolved. After some convincing from Ilsa-vyx--who could tell that somewhere deep down Kiarin was uncertain about the Hidden Lord--which seemed to persuade her. Then, unbeknownst to the rest of the party, Karnivash cast a suggestion over Kiarin, bending her to his will. Mekhi picked up on this with his oracular sense from his storm rune and bucked the car, sending everyone flying into the air for a moment. Unfortunately, this wasn't enough to prevent the spell from taking hold.

Mekhi then used Giant's Might, launching himself out of the driver's seat to grapple the Hidden Lord, wrenching it away from Kiarin. He tossed it out the vehicle, and Kiarin chased after him, before which Evryn charmed Kiarin and got the Staff from her.

Returning, Evryn dispelled the charm and Kiarin gained just a tiny bit more clarity, resisting Karnivash's suggestion just enough to stop herself from hurting Evryn. The party spoke for a while longer, Kiarin lashing out at the rest of the party. She dismissed Mekhi as an untrustworthy [[Demons|Demon]]-consorting liar, rudely sidestepped Ilsa-vyx's calls for democracy and reason, and leveraged Evryn's guilt for momentarily charming her to convince him to hand over the staff. Even still, Evryn was uncertain about what was the right thing to do, but agreed with Kiarin that it wasn't his decision to begin with.

With several burned bridges, Kiarin touched the Staff to the Hidden Lord, and a hand rose from it. The shield became inert as a humanoid emerged. A pale skinned thing with large claws and curled horns, marked with Abyssal iconography. [[Lord Yashio]].
